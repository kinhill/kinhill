About the Workshop

For a lot of people the idea of food fermenting can seem scary, but it’s actually our oldest tool to make food last longer, taste better, and actually be safer!

Join us for a hands-on exploration of the microbial world. A sensory deep-dive into the ancient art of lacto-fermentation and probiotic drinks.

The Day

Sensory Awakening: Experience the fizz, the pop, and the vibrant colors of active ferments.

From Garden to Studio: Harvest fresh produce and get hands-on packing your own jars.

Science in Action: Demystify the invisible work of Lactobacillus and the symbiotic dance of the SCOBY.

What You’ll Take Home

  • • Your own hand-crafted vegetable ferments.
  • • Technical knowledge to ferment safely at home.
  • • Access to our online community forum.
